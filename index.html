Wand Scan

Scan documents, posters or any flat surface with your iPhone camera. First mark the four corners of the area by hand, then tap Start scan and slowly move the phone over the surface. The app decides on its own when to take each shot (sharp, steady, and covering new area) and stitches them into one image. Tap Snap now any time to force a shot within a few seconds.

  • Set the four corners first. Hold the phone at a comfortable height of about 15–25 cm and put the on-screen target over the top-left corner of your area, then tap Place corner 1. Move the phone (slowly, same height) to the top-right, bottom-right and bottom-left corners and tap the button at each. Use Undo corner if you misplace one. Then tap Start scan.
  • The top-left corner is your home: if tracking is ever lost, return to it and the scan carries on.
  • Sweep slowly, like painting. For big things do rows or columns in a snake pattern and overlap each pass by about 30–50%. There's no size limit while scanning.
  • Go closer for detail. Closer shots are higher resolution and replace blurrier or farther ones, as long as the camera can still focus (about 12 cm minimum).
  • Keep the phone roughly parallel to the surface.
  • The tracker needs visible texture such as print, pictures or a patterned table. Plain white paper alone can't be tracked.
  • Use bright, even light and avoid your own shadow. Safari can't switch the flashlight on from a web page, so use a lamp or the Control Center flashlight.
  • This page must be opened over https for the camera to work.
