Wand Scan

Turn your iPhone into a flatbed scanner. Hold it flat over a page, calibrate the height, then sweep slowly in rows or columns. The app tracks the surface and stitches everything into one image.

  • Best height: about 15–25 cm. Lower is sharper, but closer than ~12 cm the iPhone can't focus.
  • Hold the phone parallel to the surface. Tilting breaks the stitching.
  • The tracker needs visible texture: print, pictures, a patterned table. Blank white paper alone can't be tracked, so leave some table or other texture in view.
  • Sweep slowly (like painting). Do rows in a snake pattern and overlap each new row about 30–50% with the last.
  • Use bright, even light and avoid casting your own shadow. Safari can't switch the flashlight on from a web page, so use a lamp or Control Center flashlight.
  • Camera needs a secure (https) page.
